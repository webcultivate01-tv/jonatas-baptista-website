Every role taught me something different. Every lesson influences the way I coach today.

Real estate taught me that every transaction begins with people — not property. Building trust, understanding motivations, and communicating clearly became the foundation of everything I would do next.

2005 – 2015

Leading agents gave me a new perspective. I realised success wasn't determined by who worked the hardest. It belonged to those with the right habits, better decisions, and a consistent approach to growth.

2016 – 2018

As my responsibilities grew, so did my understanding of business performance. I saw how leadership, strategy, and clear direction could transform not only individuals but entire teams.

2019 – 2025

Understanding behaviour, communication, and decision-making opened a completely different perspective. I learned that every conversation influences trust, every message shapes perception, and every decision affects results.

Today

Client Acquisition Coach
